# Attribution and data credits

SkyFlip uses publicly available astronomy data. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by NASA, JPL, Caltech, or IPAC beyond the use of their open archives.

## NASA / JPL-Caltech / IPAC

- **SPHEREx** Level-2 Spectral Image products and Quick Release / DR1 archives hosted by **IRSA (IPAC)**.
- Please credit: *NASA / JPL-Caltech / IPAC* and the SPHEREx mission team when redistributing images or derived products.
- Archive: https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/

## Catalogs and services

- **SIMBAD** (CDS) for name → coordinate resolution.
- **Gaia** and **AllWISE** catalogs (via IRSA / VO services) for overlays and cross-matching.
- **JPL Horizons** and **JPL Small-Body** services for solar-system ephemerides when used.

## Software

- Astropy, astroquery, NumPy, SciPy, FastAPI, React, Vite, and other open-source libraries under their respective licenses.

## Discovery claims

SkyFlip does **not** claim to discover Planet X or any new solar-system object. Detected movers are labeled as **candidates** unless cross-matched to a known catalog object.
